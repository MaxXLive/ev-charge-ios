# Privacy Policy — EVMap for iOS

**Last updated: 2026-06-13**

EVMap for iOS does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data you enter

- **Location**: used only on-device to center the map and sort favorites by distance. Never transmitted to the developer.
- **Tesla account credentials**: if you sign in to your Tesla account, credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only, used solely to fetch real-time Supercharger availability, and never transmitted to the developer.
- **Filter settings and favorites**: stored locally on your device via SwiftData.

## Third-party services

EVMap contacts the following external APIs to load charging station data. Your IP address is visible to these services as part of normal network requests:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| GoingElectric | Charging station data for DACH | goingelectric.de |
| Open Charge Map | Charging station data worldwide | openchargemap.org |
| Nobil | Charging station data for Norway/Sweden | nobil.no |
| EnBW | Real-time availability for GoingElectric stations | enbw.com |
| Chargeprice | Price comparison (deep-link only, no data sent) | chargeprice.app |

## Analytics

No analytics, tracking SDKs, or crash reporting tools are included.
