EV Charge helps you find electric vehicle charging stations from multiple data sources: GoingElectric, Open Charge Map, and Nobil. All in one ad-free, open source app.

FEATURES
- Multiple data sources: combine GoingElectric (DACH), Open Charge Map (worldwide), and Nobil (Norway/Sweden)
- Map with pins colored by power, clustering, and standard, satellite or hybrid views
- Station details: connectors with type and power, opening hours, costs, swipeable photos, charge cards
- Real-time availability for GoingElectric stations in the DACH/EU region via the EnBW API
- Tesla Superchargers: real-time availability, pricing and utilization after signing in, no Tesla vehicle required
- Powerful filters: connector type, power, network, charge cards, accessibility and more
- Filter profiles: save and load your own presets
- Favorites: bookmark stations and sort by distance
- Apple CarPlay: find charging stations right on your car's display
- Search for places and find charging stations nearby via Apple Maps
- Translate station notes with Apple's native Translation framework

A native iOS app for finding EV charging stations. Ad-free, non-commercial, open source. Source code: https://github.com/MaxXLive/ev-charge-ios

Based on EVMap by Johan von Forstner (MIT License).

EV Charge is not an official product of GoingElectric.de, Open Charge Map, or Nobil; it only uses their public APIs.
